# Financial research workspace

This workspace is for market research: explaining price moves in stocks, ETFs, indices and crypto, and researching listed companies.

For any question about a price or a price move, a company, earnings, filings or exchange announcements, analyst actions, valuations, or a crypto pair, use the `financial-research` skill before answering, even when a quick web search looks enough. Read `.claude/skills/financial-research/SKILL.md`, follow it, and run its `scripts/fin.py`.
